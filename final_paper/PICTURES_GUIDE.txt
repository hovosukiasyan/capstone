  ──────────────────────
  What:   Optuna trial history plot for the GBM study.
          X-axis = trial number (0–49), Y-axis = CV R² score.
  Where:  Look for the cell that calls optuna.visualization.plot_optimization_history()
          or a manual plt scatter of trial values.
  Export: plt.savefig('optuna-gb-history.png', dpi=150, bbox_inches='tight')

  gbm-residual-vs-actual.png
  ───────────────────────────
  What:   Predicted vs actual household income for the tuned GBM.
          Log-log scale recommended (income spans 4 orders of magnitude).
          X-axis = actual income (AMD, log scale)
          Y-axis = predicted income (AMD, log scale)
          Ideal: include a y=x reference line.
  Where:  The cell after the final GBM evaluation block.
  Export: plt.savefig('gbm-residual-vs-actual.png', dpi=150, bbox_inches='tight')
