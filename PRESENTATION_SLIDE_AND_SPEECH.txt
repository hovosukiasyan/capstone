WHAT TO SPEAK — Full Speech Guide
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

OPENING (15 seconds):
"Armenia's official poverty statistics come out once a year. By the time
they're published, it's already too late to act on them. So the question I
built this project around is: can we predict those numbers before they
arrive, with nothing but the previous year's data?"

SETUP — why it's hard (20 seconds):
"We have 11 regions — marzes — and only 7 years of data per region,
from 2016 to 2022. That's the entire panel. Neural networks normally
need hundreds of data points to learn patterns. With 7, they overfit
immediately and actually performed worse than a trivial baseline. So
I went back to basics."

THE APPROACH (20 seconds):
"The simplest possible model — Lag-1 — just says: next year's poverty
rate equals this year's. That's it. No parameters. No training.
I also built a Ridge autoregressive model that uses the last two years
plus a stress index combining crime rates and healthcare access.
Then I combined them 50/50 into an Ensemble."

THE HONEST TEST (30 seconds):
"Here's where it gets interesting. I didn't evaluate any of these models
on training data. I trained everything on 2016 through 2021 — and then
looked at 2022 for the first time, after training was complete. That's the
bar chart you see here. Real predictions against real 2022 ArmStat data.
Lag-1 achieves a regional mean absolute error of 5.07 percentage points
across all 11 marzes. And the Ensemble — the average of Lag-1 and Ridge —
gets the national-level poverty rate right to within 0.02 percentage points.
Armenia's actual 2022 national poverty rate was 24.3%. The Ensemble
predicted 24.28%. That's not luck — that's directional errors cancelling
under population weighting."

WHAT FEATURE IMPORTANCE TOLD US (20 seconds):
"On the household side, I trained five models on the 2015 ILCS survey —
5,184 households. The clearest finding from feature importance: the single
strongest predictor of whether a household is poor isn't what they own —
it's how many income sources they have. Diversification matters more than
assets. Households relying on one income stream — a single pension or
wage — are the most vulnerable."

CLOSING (15 seconds):
"Everything I just described is live on a public dashboard where you can
explore any marz, compare models, and see the 2023-to-2026 projections.
The biggest limitation is honest: 7 data points per region is a structural
constraint no amount of modeling can fully overcome. But within that
constraint, a simple validated pipeline can produce actionable regional
estimates — and that's the contribution."

TOTAL ESTIMATED SPEAKING TIME: ~2 minutes
